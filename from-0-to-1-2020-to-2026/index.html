From 0 to 1 / 2020 to 2026

Nils November 21, 2026

When I was leaving my little hometown to escape depression and aimlessness, I moved to Nürnberg with my equally depressed, first-ever girlfriend. My parents, typically overworked and aspirational conservatives from a Brandenburg small town, helped me move, and so I was left in my first apartment away from home, repeating the same patterns I had learned all my life by observing.

In a short span, life began to deteriorate as the learned patterns broke, since the only one holding me accountable was myself. My studies deteriorated because of my arrogant learning mindset, the apartment fell into disrepair, my body deteriorated, and my constructed worldview fell into chaos and confusion.

All of this was like a madhouse. My parents backed me financially, my CV had no gaps, and all seemed normal on paper. It was a full crisis nonetheless.

In retrospect, I was running from myself and living with a deeply manifested self-hatred and loathing for life.
I was aimless and held onto anything that seemed sturdy enough to hold me for a few days.
This time was the beginning of a steep and steady slide down to the rock bottom of my life until then.

In superspeed, life turned itself on its head. I met my fiancée, and it changed my worldview and opened the door to a world I hadn't known before. Since the beginning of our relationship, we were deeply passionate and had all the quarrels of love and all the spikes of joy. We moved in together, her dog joined after a year, and then the moment of my biggest growth period started.

Mid-2024, we moved to Berlin after our apartment got moldy by faulty construction. We packed up our stuff and went.
We drained my bank account and found a place we couldn't furnish because we had no money, and all our dreams of a better life were on the floor.
We arrived in the city with no income, no friends, and no idea what to do next.
Then it finally struck me: I never had an idea to begin with.

The first Christmas passed quickly. We made friends, built up our lives, and just struggled to keep our necks above the water.
(I made a lot of bad choices which brought me to my own brink of collapse: I turned numb, just functioned, and preserved my energy.) I was getting hit seemingly from every direction, and it didn't seem to stop, and I was just lost and desperate.

Again I grappled onto anything I could to stabilize myself. I went to a therapist, read the Bible, listened to stoic philosophy and alpha male propaganda, and wanted to search for what was right, relevant, and worth living for. I was always searching, always dissatisfied with what I found, and always longing.

Then one day, as I was still sitting lost and full of regrets, a spark of fight came up in me, and I realized through different connections that life is not about finding or chasing something. It was about presence in the moment and living life as it happens, with intentionality and gratitude.
This was brought to me by listening to The Bible Project podcast, stoic philosophy, and the YouTube channel Reflections on Life. That was a starting point at which I realized a profound truth:
I had very little time on this planet, and I had just gained consciousness after 20% of it had already been spent shaping my identity, my way of thinking, and experiencing.

Suddenly, I had a calm determination. No more wasted time. Suddenly I went from -1 to 0. That was the beginning of 2025, around February. I changed my job from an assistant at an insurance agency and became a sales rep at a SaaS company.
It was a pivotal moment where I took the initiative in life. I was taking my fate and happiness into my own hands. I became proactive.

I quickly changed jobs again to a sales role at an AI company, where I had full responsibility over my area, my quota, and my pipeline. Now I am at 1, at full speed to go to 100. Not to be anywhere or to get somewhere, but to be 100% present in the now and live.

"it is not that we have a short time to live, but that we waste a lot of it. Life is long enough, and a sufficiently generous amount has been given to us for the highest achievements if it were all well invested."

Seneca, de brevitate vitae

 

Now it is the end of November 2026. It took two years to go from my deepest point in life so far to a step into life itself.

No matter where you stand in life right now, think and realize: you are standing with your feet in your own life. Take charge of it and be proactive. Make life the sum of your proactive choices. Live well, and you will live long enough, no matter how short it may be in years.
