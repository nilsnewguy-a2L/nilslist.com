Why this, Why now?

Nils November 20, 2026

What is "nilslist" and why does it exist? 

nilslist is the outcome of my thought to give back to the world what I have received: a helping hand with a bit of the right information at the right time. It will start with a few ideas and grow with time through the engagement of readers and other contributors.

What is it actually about? 

Life is difficult and complicated when you have no idea what is going on. An individual suffers through this state and falls into addiction, dependencies, or negative thought patterns if they are overwhelmed and do not have the right tools at hand.

For me, the right tools came through searching—searching through countless websites, blogs, videos, philosophies, and so on. It was long, painful, and, in retrospect, aimless and ridiculous.

I thought that since I suffered and learned through all these free resources, others who don't have the energy to search and forage are in the same need of information. So, simply put, I took matters into my own hands, utilized Claude, and put together this platform to provide good materials for finding a better perspective on life and lessening the hardship of interested readers, free and available, just as I found them myself.

This project will help me find my way and hopefully be useful to you, too. Share what you find valuable, and contribute what you find meaningful.

Best, Nils
